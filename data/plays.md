Basketball 1.0

•	Horns – Two big players stand at the elbows, the point guard uses one of them for a screen, and the offense plays from there.
•	Flex – A player cuts along the baseline using a screen, then another teammate comes off a down screen for a shot or drive.
•	Miami – One player sets a ball screen, then another teammate immediately screens for the screener to help them get open to the basket.
•	Spain – A player sets a ball screen, another teammate back screens the screener’s defender, then pops out for an open shot.
•	Spain leak - The back screener does not stay in the action but leaks out to space the floor (weak side corner) 
•	Spain P&R - in addition to the normal ball screen, the third offensive player sets a back screener on the defender of the roller 
•	Chin – The point guard passes to the wing, cuts through using a screen, and then a ball screen creates options for a layup, drive, or open jumper.
•	Rub – An offensive player (big) cuts very close to a teammate(guard) from the top of the key, to use them like a screen and shake their defender.
•	Pin Down – A player facing the basket, sets a screen for a teammate moving from the paint up toward the wing or top of the key.
•	Turn – The screener changes the angle of the screen by pivoting after the defender’s position changes to create a better driving lane. 
•	Stagger – off ball action where A player runs off two screens set one after the other to get open for a shot, drive, or catch.
•	UCLA Cut - Perimeter to post action where guard passes to wing and then cuts to the rim off a back screen at the high post (elbow) looking for layup or catch
•	Twirl: stagger variation where the cutter comes only off of the first screen and then “splits the stagger” instead of continuing
•	Shallow cut: Perimeter cut where player changes sides, running across the top of the floor and staying outside the 3pt line, often going under a potential/existing ball screen
•	Weave - continuous perimeter passes/hamdoffs 
•	Shuffle screen - off ball screen, near the line, freeing the cutter to make diagonal cut towards rim
•	Pitch Back – A player passes to a teammate, then quickly gets the ball back with a return pass to create an advantage for a shot, drive, or ball screen.
•	Stagger Entry – The offense starts by passing to a player coming off two back-to-back screens to get the ball into the half-court offense with an advantage.
•	Ghost screen - Fake on ball screen, with screener quickly slipping to space 
•	Gut - Vertical cut or action through the middle lane , from under the basket toward the top/slot
•	Ram - off ball screen set first to free a player who immediately comes to set a ball screen
•	Zoom – A player comes off a pin-down screen and immediately uses a handoff from a teammate, creating a quick driving or shooting opportunity.
•	Euro Flow – An offense that flows continuously from one action to the next (screens, handoffs, and pick-and-rolls) without stopping, so the defense is always forced to react.
•	Wide Pin Down to Curl – A player sets a wide pin-down screen, and the cutter curls tightly around it toward the basket to catch the ball on the move for a layup or short shot.
•	BLOB - Baseline out of bounds play, when offense inbounds under the basket 
•	Flare screen is a screen set to send a teammate away from the ball toward the wing or corner, helping them get open for a catch-and-shoot three or attack against a closeout.
•	Flex Screen to Screener – A flex screen is a baseline screen that frees a teammate cutting to the basket; after setting it, the screener immediately uses another screen to get open as the next scoring option.
•	Floppy - Shooter starts under the basket and uses a single or stagger screen to come out to the perimeter for catch and shoot 
•	Grenade - low post player steps out from the block while a guard cuts toward the block to receive a DHO
•	Split - Off ball action after post entry or HO, where two perimeter players near the ball, set it fake a screen to create opportunity, while ball is in the post or elbow
